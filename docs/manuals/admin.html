1. What the dashboard does

Doctors use the OMP DigiCoach app on their phones while they teach. Each time they teach a learner, the app records a session: the learner, the case, the time taken and a rating from 1 to 5 for each of the five teaching steps.

The dashboard is where you, the admin, see all of this. You can:

  • add doctors and give them their first password
  • reset a password when a doctor forgets it
  • switch off a doctor who has left
  • see every doctor, student and session
  • print reports and download all the data for analysis

The five teaching steps

Ratings on the dashboard always follow these five steps, in this order:

StepNameWhat the doctor does
1Get a CommitmentAsks the learner for a diagnosis or plan
2Probe for EvidenceExplores the learner’s reasoning
3Teach General RuleTeaches one or two key points
4Reinforce StrengthsPraises what went well
5Correct & ImproveGuides improvement

The rating scale

RatingMeaning
1Needs improvement
2Basic
3Competent
4Proficient
5Excellent

A dash (–) anywhere on the dashboard means “not recorded”, for example a step the doctor did not rate.

2. Logging in and out

Use a computer or laptop with Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge. You get your username and first password from the technical team.

  1. Open the browser and go to ompdigicoach.com.
  2. Type your Username and Password.
  3. Click Log In. The dashboard opens.

The first time you log in

The app asks you to choose your own password. Type the temporary password, then your new password twice, and click Save new password. Use at least 6 characters. A short phrase of a few words is easy to remember, for example “tea on the ward window”.

Logging out

Click Log out at the top right. Always log out on a shared computer.

You may be asked to log in againFor safety, the dashboard logs you out after 30 minutes without use, and always after 8 hours. Just log in again; nothing is lost.

3. Finding your way around

Every page has the same layout:

  • The menu on the left takes you to the five main pages: Overview, Doctors, Students, Sessions and Export.
  • The top right shows your name and the Log out button.
  • Blue underlined names and dates are links. Click them to open that doctor, student or session.
  • In tables, click a column heading to sort by it. Click again to reverse the order.
Times are Pakistan timeAll dates and times on the dashboard, and in reports and downloads, are in Pakistan time.

6. Adding a doctor

Add each doctor once. They then log in on their own phone.

  1. Click Doctors in the menu, then + Add doctor at the top right.
  2. Name: the doctor’s full name, for example “Dr. Sara Malik”.
  3. Username: the name they will type to log in. Use small letters, numbers, dots or underscores only, 3 to 30 characters, for example dr.sara. No spaces.
  4. Role: leave it on Doctor: uses the phone app.
  5. Choose their Department and Designation.
  6. Temporary password: leave it empty and the app makes one for you. Or click Generate password to see it now.
  7. Click Add doctor.

Give the doctor their temporary password

After you click Add doctor, the temporary password appears once.

  1. Write the password down, or click Copy.
  2. Give the doctor their username and temporary password in person or by phone call.
  3. Click Done.
Never send passwords by email, WhatsApp or SMSMessages can be forwarded or read by others. Tell the doctor in person or on a call.

What the doctor does next: they open ompdigicoach.com on their phone, log in with the temporary password, and choose their own password. The app then shows them a short tour. Their status changes from “Must change password” to “Active”.

Lost the temporary password?No problem. Open the doctor, click Edit, then Reset password to get a new one (chapter 7).

Adding another admin

An admin uses only this dashboard, not the phone app. To add one, follow the same steps but choose Admin: uses this dashboard as the role. Department and designation are not needed.

One role per accountAn account is either a doctor or an admin, never both. If an admin also teaches, give them two accounts with different usernames: an admin one for the dashboard and a doctor one for the phone.

7. Looking after a doctor’s account

A doctor’s page

Click a doctor’s name in the Doctors list. Their page shows their activity at the top, then three tables.

  • Spread of ratings: for each step, how many times they gave each rating.
  • Students taught: every student they taught, with the number of sessions.
  • Sessions: all their sessions, newest first. The ratings column shows steps 1 to 5 in order, for example “4 · 3 · 5 · 4 · 2”.

Changing a doctor’s details

  1. Open the doctor and click Edit.
  2. Change the name, username, department or designation.
  3. Click Save changes.
Changed a username?Tell the doctor. They must use the new username next time they log in.

When a doctor forgets their password

  1. Open the doctor and click Edit.
  2. Click Reset password, then Reset password again to confirm.
  3. A new temporary password appears once. Give it to the doctor in person or by phone call.

The doctor is logged out on their phone and must choose a new password with the temporary one. Their sessions are safe.

Sessions waiting on the phone are keptIf the doctor recorded sessions without signal, the phone keeps them and sends them once the doctor logs in again with the new password.

When a doctor leaves: switch them off

  1. Open the doctor and click Edit.
  2. Click Switch off, then Switch off again to confirm.

They can no longer log in or send sessions. All their past sessions stay in the dashboard and in downloads. To let them back in, open the same Edit page and click Switch on.

Switch off, don’t deleteThere is no way to delete a doctor. Switching off keeps the study data complete.

You cannot switch off your own account, or remove your own admin role. This stops you locking yourself out.

8. Students

Doctors add students on their phones. All doctors share one student list, so a student taught by several doctors appears once.

The students list

Click Students in the menu.

  • Search: type part of a name or a PMDC number in the search box.
  • Sort by: Name, Most sessions, Most recent session, or PMDC number.
  • Doctors: how many different doctors have taught this student.

A student’s page

Click a student’s name. The top shows their number of sessions and doctors, and their average for each step.

Correcting a student’s details

If a doctor typed a name or PMDC number wrongly, fix it in the Profile box: change the name, PMDC number or level (and year, for medical students), then click Save correction. The change is recorded in the Change history at the bottom of the page and reaches the doctors’ phones.

9. Sessions

Click Sessions in the menu to see every session from every doctor, newest first.

Finding sessions

  1. Choose any of: a From date, a To date, a Doctor, a Case type.
  2. Click Filter. The number of matching sessions shows under the page title.
  3. Click Clear to see all sessions again.
ColumnMeaning
TeachingTotal teaching time
ExtraTime past the 1-minute target
Ratings, steps 1–5The five ratings in order. A dash means that step was not rated.
UsefulHow useful the doctor found the teaching, from 1 to 6

One session

Click a session’s date to see everything recorded: doctor, student, case, times, the doctor’s notes and the rating for each step.

Deleting a session

Only delete a session that was recorded by mistake, for example a test.

  1. Open the session and click Delete session.
  2. Click Delete session again to confirm.
Deleting cannot be undoneThe session and its ratings are removed from the dashboard and downloads. The deletion is recorded. The doctor still sees it in the History on their own phone.

10. Reports and printing

There are two printable reports: one for a student and one for a doctor.

Student report

  1. Open the student (Students, then click their name).
  2. Click Report at the top right.
  3. To show only part of the time, choose a From and To date. Leave them empty for all sessions.
  4. Click Print or save as PDF.

Doctor report

Open the doctor, click Report, then Print or save as PDF. It shows their activity, the spread of their ratings and their sessions.

Saving as a PDF fileIn the print window, set Destination (or Printer) to Save as PDF, then click Save. The menu and buttons are left out of the printout automatically.

11. Downloading the data (Export)

Export gives you a spreadsheet file (CSV) with one row per session. It opens in Excel and SPSS.

  1. Click Export in the menu.
  2. Choose a From and To date, or leave both empty to download every session.
  3. Click Download CSV. The file goes to your Downloads folder.
Keep downloaded files safeThe file contains student names and ratings. Store it only on a password-protected computer and do not email it. Each download is recorded.

13. Problems and answers

I forgot my own password.

Ask another admin to reset it for you (chapter 7). If you are the only admin, contact the technical team.

The dashboard sent me back to the login page.

You were logged out for safety after 30 minutes without use, or after 8 hours. Log in again.

A doctor says “Wrong username or password”.

Check the username on their Doctors page; it must be typed exactly, in small letters. If they still cannot log in, reset their password.

A doctor says “This account has been switched off”.

Open their Edit page and click Switch on.

A doctor’s session is not on the dashboard.

Sessions recorded without signal wait on the phone. Ask the doctor to open the app where there is signal. The badge at the top of their app shows “All sent” when everything has arrived.

The same student appears twice.

Two doctors probably added them with slightly different names. Correct the details on one of them so they are easy to tell apart, and tell the doctors which one to use.

“That username is taken.”

Someone already has it. Choose another, for example add the first letter of the surname: dr.sara.m.

A number looks wrong.

Averages leave out steps that were not rated. Check the session list for that doctor or student, and remember dates are Pakistan time.
